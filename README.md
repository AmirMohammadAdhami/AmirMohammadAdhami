## 🚀 Featured Projects


### 🩺 [Persian Medical Case Report Translator](https://github.com/AmirMohammadAdhami/persian-medical-case-report-translator)

A CLI tool that translates English medical & dental case reports into fluent, professional Persian — without corrupting clinical meaning.

- **Column-aware PDF parsing** — detects full-width bands and two-column sections, merges broken lines, de-hyphenates
- **Anatomical names intentionally stay in English** (e.g. `maxillary right lateral incisor`) to avoid real clinical errors
- **Paired terminology** rendered as `شکستگی پیچیده تاج-ریشه (Complicated Crown-Root Fracture)`, with research-backed clickable glossary entries
- **Figures preserved** at their exact logical position in the reading flow
- **Optional AI Vision** (`--enable-vision`) describes radiographs and clinical photos without hallucinating
- Elegant **RTL, print-ready HTML** output · 108 tests passing
- Multi-provider: OpenCode Zen, Google Gemini, OpenAI, with automatic offline mock fallback

`Python 3.13` `PyMuPDF` `OpenAI-compatible SDK` `pytest`
